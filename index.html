World Wide Energy Consumption

A MySQL project across six related tables for energy production, consumption, emissions, GDP, and population, answering 15 questions on trends, ratios, and global comparisons.

Analysis highlights

Across the common 2020-2023 emissions period, global emissions rose ~9.29% (33,927.61 to 37,079.18 MMtonnes CO₂). Coal and coke contributed 44.81%; petroleum and liquids 33.14%; natural gas 22.05%. The six tables are country, consumption, production, emission_3, gdp_3, and population. The analysis uses CTEs, window functions, per-capita calculations, and GDP/emissions comparisons.
